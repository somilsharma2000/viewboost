## 3. Watch-to-earn / GPT landscape in India

- Massive reach in Tier 2/3/4 cities; viewers seek ₹20–₹100/day supplementary income.
- The market is **low-trust**: high withdrawal thresholds (₹100–₹500), hidden fees, delayed payouts, silent account bans.
- **Opening:** zero-threshold-feeling instant UPI payouts + transparent immutable ledger + human-review appeals = the exact opposite of every incumbent's reputation.

## 4. The ToS reality (read before selling anything)

YouTube's Fake Engagement Policy:

> "YouTube doesn't allow anything that artificially increases the number of views, likes, comments, or other metrics either by using automatic systems or serving up videos to unsuspecting viewers. Also, content that solely exists to incentivize viewers for engagement (views, likes, comments, etc.) is prohibited."

**Consequences for channels caught with incentivized traffic:** view scrubbing, YPP rejection/termination, channel strikes.

**How competitors frame it:**
- Ad agencies (VeeFly, Sprizzy): "We only use official Google Ads — 100% safe."
- SMM panels: "Real human views, non-drop" + "use at your own risk, we're not responsible for YouTube actions."

**Recommended ViewBoost framing:** position as a **consumer feedback / viewer research marketplace** — "get real audience feedback and retention heatmaps from verified human viewers," with embedded players and mandatory post-watch micro-polls. Full strategy in [Positioning Pivot](07-positioning-pivot.md).

## 5. Five market gaps ViewBoost can own

1. **Proof-of-human attention engine** — 80% completion + tab visibility + random mid-video attention checks (timestamp prompts / 1-question content quizzes). Nobody verifies attention this hard.
2. **Creator analytics & retention heatmaps** — SMM panels give a raw counter; ViewBoost can give second-by-second drop-off curves, city/state demographics, device breakdown.
3. **Zero-friction instant UPI payouts** — micro-payouts from ₹25 via RazorpayX/Cashfree batch APIs, crediting GPay/PhonePe/Paytm in seconds.
4. **Creator↔viewer feedback loop** — creators attach micro-polls ("Rate my intro 1–5", "What should I make next?"); viewers answer to unlock the reward. Passive views become focus-group research.
5. **Compliance shield** — auto GST invoices for creators, drip-feed delivery mimicking organic growth, transparent traffic disclosure. Competitors ignore all of this.
