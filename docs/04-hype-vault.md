# 04 — The Hype Vault 💥

Crazy-but-viable stunts and viral mechanics, ordered by launch impact. Each is designed to generate proof, not just noise — ViewBoost's entire brand is *verified realness*, so every stunt must produce public evidence.

---

## Tier 1 — Launch-week stunts

### 1. The "24-Hour Monetization Speedrun" 🔴
Partner with a charismatic gaming/tech streamer. Create a brand-new YouTube channel live on stream, then document 0 subs → monetized in 24 hours using ViewBoost campaigns, with YouTube Studio analytics and ViewBoost's tab-visibility logs displayed side-by-side on screen.
**Why it works:** it's the exact outcome every struggling creator dreams of, proven in public, in real time. If the channel survives YouTube's monetization review, that's the single strongest proof asset the platform can ever own.
**Cost:** one streamer fee + one Viral-tier campaign (~₹2,500).

### 2. The ₹50,000 Anti-Bot Hacker Bounty 🛡️
Public challenge to Indian devs and ethical hackers: *"Bypass our Tab-Visibility API and canvas fingerprinting with a headless script. If your bot fakes an 80% watch without a human at the screen — ₹50,000 cash."*
Stream the attempts live. When the security holds, creators get undeniable proof that ViewBoost attention is 100% human.
**Why it works:** flips the industry's biggest shame (bots) into a public trust spectacle. Failure mode is still a win — every vulnerability found makes the platform stronger.

### 3. #InstantUPIProof ⚡
Viewers post 15-second screen recordings: watch a video → tap cashout → PhonePe/Paytm UPI notification lands within seconds.
**Incentive:** ₹10 wallet bonus per verified post with the tag. Hundreds of native proof videos = free ASO + social proof library.
**Why it works:** payout-proof content is the highest-converting ad format for earning apps — and the creators pay for it themselves (in bonuses, cheaply).

## Tier 2 — First-90-day engines

### 4. Inter-College "Watch & Earn Battleground" 🏆
DU vs. IIT Bombay vs. Anna University — live public dashboard tracking verified watch-hours per campus. Winning college's E-Cell/fest gets a **₹50,000 official sponsorship**.
**Why it works:** college rivalry + free money + institutional pride = self-propagating WhatsApp word-of-mouth. Recruits thousands of verified viewers (supply side) while the brand trend-setter demographic watches.

### 5. "Zero-to-Hero" Creator Incubator (documentary series)
Sponsor 10 under-1,000-sub creators with full Viral-tier campaigns. Film a 4-part docu-series following their 30-day trajectories: subscriber gains, comments, the algorithmic push from initial view velocity.
**Why it works:** underdog stories are the most shareable creator-economy content, and the series doubles as evergreen conversion material.

## Tier 3 — Crazy future ideas (the vault)

### 6. "Watch-to-Mint" attention NFTs
Each verified watch session generates a cryptographic "Proof-of-Attention" token (hash of session telemetry, creator, timestamp). Not currency — **receipts**. Creators can publicly prove campaign authenticity; top viewers collect rare badges. Makes "verified human attention" a brandable artifact.

### 7. The Attention Exchange (long game)
Open a public API where any app can rent ViewBoost's verification engine — dating apps verifying profile viewership, ed-tech verifying lecture attendance, brands verifying ad attention. ViewBoost becomes **proof-of-attention infrastructure**, not a view shop. This is the unicorn story (see [roadmap](05-roadmap-unicorn.md)).

### 8. "Second Screen" live prediction games
During the Speedrun-style livestreams, viewers predict retention-curve outcomes of live campaigns. Correct predictions earn wallet credit. Turns ViewBoost's analytics into entertainment.

### 9. Creator "Attention Audit" reports as content
Auto-generate a shareable card for every completed campaign: retention curve, best 5 seconds, worst drop-off second, audience cities. Creators share these like Spotify-Wrapped — free viral distribution with ViewBoost branding on every card.

### 10. The ₹1 Lakh "Watch India Watch" day
One day, one flagship campaign: the first 100,000 people to complete a verified watch of a national-day creator anthem split ₹1,00,000 via UPI. Live counter on national news bait. A single-day supply-side supercharge and a press magnet.

---

## Rules of the vault
1. **Every stunt must produce public proof** — screenshots, streams, ledgers. No proof, no point.
2. **Never fake it.** One staged payout or bot-view scandal ends the entire brand; the honest-differentiator position is the moat.
3. **Stunts recruit supply (viewers) first, demand (creators) second** — liquidity is always the binding constraint.
